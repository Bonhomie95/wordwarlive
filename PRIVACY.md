# WordWar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-11 · **DRAFT — review with legal counsel before publishing.**_

This is a starting-point policy that reflects what the app actually collects
based on the codebase. You must host it at a public URL and link it in App
Store Connect, Google Play, and the AdMob console before launch.

## Who we are
WordWar ("we", "the app") is a real-time word game published by Bonhomie Inc.
Contact: **[your support email]**.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** a username you choose, and — depending on sign-in method —
  your email address (email sign-in) or an opaque provider identifier (Sign in
  with Apple / Google). Anonymous play uses a randomly generated device
  identifier, not your name or email.
- **Gameplay data:** match results, rank, coins, cosmetics, streaks, and
  in-game purchases, used to run the game and leaderboards.
- **Purchase validation:** store-issued purchase receipts and transaction IDs,
  used solely to verify and de-duplicate in-app purchases. We do **not** receive
  or store your card number or full payment details — those stay with Apple /
  Google.
- **Advertising:** we use Google AdMob, which may collect a device advertising
  identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. On iOS we request
  App Tracking Transparency permission first. See Google's policies at
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
- **Diagnostics:** if enabled, aggregated crash and performance data to fix bugs.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone.

## Legal bases / your rights
You can **delete your account and all associated data at any time** from
Settings → Account → Delete account (or by contacting support). This permanently
removes your profile, rank, coins, cosmetics, and match history. You may also
request a copy of your data by contacting us.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in
your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Data retention
Account data is retained until you delete your account. Anonymized/aggregate
analytics may be retained longer.

## Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be surfaced in-app or at the
hosted policy URL.
