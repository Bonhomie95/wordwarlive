# WordWar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

WordWar ("the app", "we") is a real-time word game published by Bonhomie Inc.
This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to exercise your rights.
Contact: **adeyemibabatundejoseph@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

- **Account data.** A username you choose. Depending on how you sign in: your
  email address (email sign-in), or an opaque identifier from Google or Apple
  (Sign in with Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real
  email). Guest play uses a random identifier generated on your device — no
  name or email.
- **Gameplay data.** Match results, guesses, rank, coins, cosmetics, streaks,
  battle-pass progress, friends, blocks, and reports you file. We need this to
  run the game, leaderboards, and moderation.
- **Purchase verification.** Store-issued purchase tokens / signed transactions
  and transaction IDs, used only to verify and de-duplicate in-app purchases.
  We never receive your card number or payment details — those stay with Apple
  or Google.
- **Push notifications.** If you allow notifications, a push token for your
  device so friends can challenge you while the app is closed. You can revoke
  this in your device settings at any time.
- **Advertising.** The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may
  collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse
  location to serve and measure ads. On iOS we ask for App Tracking
  Transparency permission first; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. In
  the EEA/UK and some US states we show a consent form and honor your choice
  (you can change it in Settings → Privacy options). See
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and
  https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.
- **Diagnostics.** Server logs (IP address, request path, timing) kept briefly
  for security and debugging. If crash reporting is enabled, anonymized crash
  data.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not collect contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or health data.
- Power-ups and ranking are never sold; matches are scored on our servers.

## Third parties we share data with

Only what each needs to provide its service: Apple (Sign in with Apple, App
Store purchases), Google (Google Sign-In, Google Play purchases, AdMob ads),
Expo (push-notification delivery). Our servers and database are hosted with
a cloud provider under standard data-processing terms.

## Your rights and choices

- **Delete your account** at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account
  → Delete account. Or request deletion on the web via the
  [account deletion page](/legal/delete-account). Deletion permanently removes
  your profile, rank, coins, cosmetics, friends, and match history.
- **Access / correction.** Email us to request a copy of your data.
- **Ads.** iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking. Android: Settings → Google →
  Ads. In-app: Settings → Privacy options (where consent applies).
- **Notifications.** Turn off in your device settings.

Residents of the EEA/UK, California, and other regions with privacy laws have
additional rights (access, portability, objection, non-discrimination). Email
us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

WordWar is not directed to children under 13 (or the digital-consent age in
your region) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child
has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Server logs are retained
for a limited period. Aggregate, anonymized statistics may be kept longer.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and sessions
can be revoked from Settings → Log out of all devices.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes are surfaced in the app or at this
URL, with the date above updated.
